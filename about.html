How I work

Follow the system across its interfaces.

I enjoy problems where firmware, circuits, mechanics, data, and human interaction all have to agree. Recent work has taken me from STM32 motor drivers and flight-control software to a native iOS product and AI-assisted operating workflows.

That range has made me especially attentive to clear boundaries: what a component owns, how it fails, how it can be tested, and how another person understands it.
