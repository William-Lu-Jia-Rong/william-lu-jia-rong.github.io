About Me

Hello! I’m Jiarong (Bill) Lu, currently pursuing a Bachelor of Applied Science in Computer Engineering at the University of Waterloo (2024-2029). My academic journey is driven by a passion for embedded systems, robotics, and AI-driven solutions.

I have hands-on experience in embedded firmware development, PCB design, and technical project management, gained through my work with the Waterloo Aerial Robotics Group, Mindmatrix Technology, and various personal and team projects. I enjoy building things that bridge the gap between hardware and software, and I thrive in collaborative, innovative environments.

My goal is to leverage technology to solve real-world problems and make a meaningful impact. Outside of academics, I enjoy video and audio production, creative web development, and supporting technical teams for events.
