Concept: Education and networking platform for students and job seekers.

Approach: Full-stack implementation with AI-powered support and structured data workflows.

Project Summary

BridgeNet was designed as a platform that blends educational support, networking, and AI-assisted guidance into one product experience. The work focused on both system design and implementation discipline, making the platform useful for real student and career journeys rather than just a demo.

Highlights

  • Architected the platform around clear user groups such as high school students, university students, and job seekers.
  • Used modern frontend and backend technologies to support scalable application flows and structured data management.
  • Integrated AI features to improve guidance, discovery, and overall user value.
