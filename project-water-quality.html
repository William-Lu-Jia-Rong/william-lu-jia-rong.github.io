Automatic Water Quality Monitor

Overview: A cost-effective water quality monitoring device using TDS sensors and STM32 microcontrollers, designed for real-time water analysis and data collection.

Technologies Used

  • STM32 Microcontroller (C/C++)
  • TDS Sensors
  • 3D Modeling & Printing (Autodesk Inventor)
  • Embedded Systems Design

Responsibilities & Highlights

  • Designed and implemented embedded firmware for sensor data acquisition and processing.
  • Developed 3D models for device housing and managed 3D printing and assembly.
  • Integrated hardware and software for real-time water quality monitoring.
  • Enabled efficient, low-cost water analysis for practical applications.
