Shanghai Mindmatrix Technology · Jun–Sep 2025

Motor control designed across firmware, PCB, and mechanics.

At Mindmatrix, I worked on the complete BLDC control path: board constraints, STM32 firmware, real-time signals, physical load testing, and the control algorithms needed for precise motion.

01 · Electronics

Compact multilayer driver hardware.

Designed and fabricated custom multilayer BLDC driver PCBs in Altium Designer, coordinated manufacturing with JLCPCB, and redesigned the board to a compact 1.8 × 3.0 cm footprint.

02 · Firmware

Real-time STM32 motor control.

Developed C/C++ firmware with PWM, ADC, and communication flows in STM32CubeIDE, using an STM32F446 for brushless motor angle detection and three-phase output control.

03 · Validation

A physical torque-testing rig.

Built a mechanical rig in Autodesk Inventor to evaluate motor performance under controlled load conditions and connect control behavior to physical results.

04 · Precision target

FOC and tuned PID loops.

Worked toward field-oriented control and finely tuned PID loops with a target of less than 0.1° angular-positioning error.

Engineering principle

Treat control as a system, not a code sample.

Precise behavior depends on the entire chain: component selection, layout, sensing, timing, mechanics, firmware, and the test process. Working across those layers made debugging more concrete and design tradeoffs easier to explain.
