# 3D asset attributions

## Bill's Workshop - original portfolio scene

- Created specifically for this portfolio; no third-party character or vehicle geometry.
- Editable source: `bill-workshop.blend`; reproducible authoring script: `build/create-workshop.py`.
- Runtime asset: `bill-workshop.glb`. Mesh materials are procedural PBR values; no external model textures required.
- Mechanical rig: named rigid transform joints with baked tool, arm, vehicle and camera tracks.
- Desktop and mobile camera tracks are included in the GLB.
- Owner may use and modify these project-created assets with the website. No claim of third-party professional commissioning is made.
- Hardware and drone geometry is illustrative, not a CAD representation of the user's confidential work.
- Product screen images retain their original ownership and are loaded separately.

The previous Poddy model below is retained as an unused historical asset.
